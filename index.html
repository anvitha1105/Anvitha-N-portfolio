01 — About

Some people freeze at a mic.
I asked for the mic.

I'm a Computer Science graduate from VIT Bhopal, but the thread running through everything I've actually done — teaching, fundraising, cloud training, content creation is the same one: taking something complicated and making it click for someone else.

I've explained Physics to a distracted 10th grader until it finally landed. I've turned a technical incident into three plain-English lines for a supervisor who didn't have time for jargon. I've written to a donor who needed to feel like their contribution mattered, because it did. Different rooms, same instinct: read the audience, translate, connect.

That instinct started early literally on stage, mic in hand, during a school assembly in Class 10 and it's been the constant across every classroom, chat window, and community I've been part of since.
